What We Build

Six Services. One Reliable Team.

From the sign above your door to the front wall it hangs on — every job is handled in-house, start to finish.

Sign Boards

3D acrylic letters, LED & non-LED sign boards with premium finishing — custom-designed for shops, restaurants, clinics and every business category.

ACP Sheet Front Elevation

Modern, long-lasting building facades finished in aluminium composite panel (ACP) sheet — clean lines, weatherproof and eye-catching from the street.

Aluminium Work

Structural framing, channel-letter boxes and aluminium fabrication built to hold your signage and elevation securely for years.

Toughened Glass

Safety-rated toughened glass for storefronts, curtain walls, railings and partitions — strength and clarity in one package.

PVC Panel Work

Wood-finish PVC wall panelling for interiors and reception areas — a premium look installed faster and cleaner than carpentry.

MS/SS Work

Mild steel & stainless steel fabrication — gates, railings, frames and structural support work, built and installed by our own team.
